# Mutqin – CME/CPD Activities Network & Operating System

## 1. One-liner

Mutqin is a CME/CPD activities network and operating system that gives health practitioners a unified digital catalogue of accredited activities, and gives authorities, providers, and execution partners a shared workflow to run those activities from concept to accredited hours.

---

## 2. Core Pillars

### 2.1 Activities Catalogue (Front End)

- Single digital home for accredited CME/CPD activities.
- Practitioners can explore activities by specialty, format, provider, date, and location.
- Past activities and upcoming opportunities are represented in a consistent, structured way.
- Feeds can include existing accredited sources and registries (e.g. legacy systems, council portals).

### 2.2 Operating System for Activities (Back End)

- Every activity lives as one structured digital record (content, sessions, speakers, documents, attendance, hours).
- Guided workflow aligns with existing CME/CPD regulations (no new rules, just cleaner execution).
- Each step has a clear owner: provider, accrediting body, execution vendor, sponsor, practitioner.
- Decisions, comments, and changes are attached to the activity timeline for future reference.

### 2.3 Authorities & Leaders Stay at the Centre

- National health councils, CME/CPD authorities, and key institutions remain the “source of truth”.
- Mutqin is the enablement layer: it carries activities to them in a structured, digital format.
- Authorities keep full control over criteria, approvals, and policies.
- Mutqin is positioned as the partner doing the heavy lifting so authorities can focus on governance and quality.

---

## 3. Activity Journey – High-Level

> 0 → Existing sources → 1–5 digital journey on Mutqin.

- **0. Activity sources**
  - Activities can flow in from existing accredited sources and registries.
  - Mutqin organises these activities into the digital catalogue to avoid a cold start.

- **1. Providers design and register**
  - CPD providers create a full activity record: content, sessions, speakers, documents.
  - Applications are submitted once in a structured format.

- **2. Accrediting bodies review and decide**
  - Authorities review the entire activity in one view.
  - Criteria and decisions are applied consistently, with traceable comments.

- **3. Providers publish and engage partners**
  - Approved activities are published to the catalogue.
  - Execution partners receive scoped access to operational details.

- **4. Event managers deliver and capture engagement**
  - Registration, check-in, attendance, and exceptions are managed digitally.
  - A reliable record of participation is created for each session.

- **5. Practitioners receive hours, authorities keep the record**
  - Practitioners receive CME/CPD hours and certificates.
  - Authorities and organisations retain structured records ready for reporting and audits.

## 4. GTM Strategy

### 4.1 Front-End First: Activities Network

- Lead with the **Activities Catalogue** as the visible front end.
- Seed the catalogue using:
  - Existing accredited activities from legacy systems and registries.
  - Selected activities from partner institutions (hospitals, councils, universities).
- Show immediate value to practitioners and providers: a live, trustworthy catalogue.

### 4.2 Strategic Anchor: SHC / National Stakeholders

- Primary GTM path:
  - Secure strategic buy-in from national bodies (e.g. councils, health commissions).
  - Position Mutqin as:
    - The digital network that organises and surfaces CME/CPD activities nationally.
    - The operating system that standardises how activities are prepared and executed.
  - Emphasis: authorities keep control; Mutqin handles structure, orchestration, and digital rails.

### 4.3 Plan B: Investor-Led + Private Sector

- In parallel, Mutqin can:
  - Attract investors aligned with healthcare and education infrastructure.
  - Run pilots with leading private hospitals, academic centres, or CPD providers.
  - Present authorities with a working model:
    - “We bring high-quality activities, ready-to-review in a structured format.”
    - “You stay in the decision seat with full visibility and traceable reports.”

### 4.4 Positioning Key Phrases (Internal Use)

- “Digital activities network and operating system for CME/CPD.”
- “Authorities stay the source of truth; Mutqin is the digital rails.”
- “We organise and deliver activities to your door, ready for your decision and oversight.”
- “Practitioners see opportunity; authorities see structure; teams see one shared reality.”

## 5. Stakeholder Roles & Outcomes

### 5.1 Authorities / Councils / National Bodies

- **What Mutqin does for them**
  - Provides a unified view of accredited and in-process activities.
  - Ensures activities arrive in a structured, complete format.
  - Maintains a clear timeline of decisions and outcomes.

- **What they keep**
  - Full control over rules, criteria, and final approvals.
  - Ability to set expectations and policies for providers and partners.

- **How they join the founding network**
  - Co-design the standard digital journey for activities.
  - Define data structures and oversight dashboards.
  - Sponsor pilots with selected providers and institutions.

### 5.2 CPD Providers

- **What Mutqin does for them**
  - One workspace to design, document, and submit activities.
  - Clear visibility on status and feedback for each activity.
  - Direct connection between their activities and the national catalogue.

- **What they keep**
  - Ownership of content, academic quality, and faculty selection.
  - Their identity and relationships with authorities and institutions.

- **How they join**
  - Onboard selected activities into Mutqin.
  - Use Mutqin templates for upcoming activities.
  - Participate in design feedback on flows and views.

### 5.3 Hospitals / Institutions

- **What Mutqin does for them**
  - Organises all CME/CPD activities that touch their staff in one place.
  - Aligns internal programs with external accreditation and hours.
  - Enables better planning and visibility on staff development.

- **What they keep**
  - Their internal education strategy and priorities.
  - Their relationships with authorities and providers.

- **How they join**
  - Pilot Mutqin for their internal activities.
  - Integrate staff rosters and attendance, where agreed.

### 5.4 Execution Vendors & Event Managers

- **What Mutqin does for them**
  - One interface for registration, attendance, and session logistics.
  - Clear handover from providers and authorities.
  - Cleaner reporting back to clients and regulators.

- **What they keep**
  - Their operational expertise and on-ground teams.
  - Their client relationships.

- **How they join**
  - Deliver selected activities through Mutqin’s operational tools.
  - Co-design check-in and attendance experiences.

### 5.5 Sponsors & Strategic Investors

- **What Mutqin does for them**
  - Provides a structured view of the CME/CPD landscape and opportunities.
  - Enables transparent, documented participation in activities.
  - Offers insight into engagement and impact.

- **What they keep**
  - Strategic direction and brand positioning.
  - Flexibility in how they support programs and ecosystem growth.

- **How they join**
  - Support pilots and launch phases.
  - Co-develop reporting views and value metrics.
